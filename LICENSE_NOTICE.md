# License and redistribution status

This archive has been **de-identified**: it contains no geographic coordinates, no real pharmacy/customer identifiers and no commercial sales. Customers and depots carry synthetic labels (`C001..`, `D01..`) and demand is given directly as integer vehicle-loads. The accompanying result files were relabelled with the same mapping, leaving every reported number unchanged.

No open-source software license was supplied with the source material; the rights holders should select one before publication. The distance/duration matrices were derived from a third-party routing service and remain subject to that provider's terms of use — confirm those conditions before redistributing the matrices. This archive **does not grant permission** for third-party redistribution, reuse or modification of the underlying data beyond what those terms allow.
